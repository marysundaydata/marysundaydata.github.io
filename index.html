Hospital analytic powerbi dashboard

Hospital Analystic dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a clear view of hospital performance and patient-related metrics. It highlights key indicators such as revenue, costs, profit, waiting time, length of stay, patient demographics, and treatment costs, with visuals that make the data easier to explore and understand. The full project details, including the data preparation and analysis process, are available on GitHub.

Logistics Operations Power BI Capstone

Logistics Operations Power BI Capstone is a four-dashboard Power BI project built from 14 logistics datasets. The dashboards explore fleet health, driver performance, customer profitability, and on-time delivery service levels. The project involved data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive data visualization to transform logistics data into meaningful operational insights. The full project, including the Power BI file and dashboard previews, is available on GitHub.

Excel customer sales dashboard

Customer sales dashboard is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to provide a clear overview of sales performance, profitability, orders, and customer activity. It highlights key metrics and allows users to explore performance across products, cities, regions, sales representatives, channels, and customer types.

About

I’m Sunday Mary Oluwaseun, an aspiring Data Analyst with a growing passion for transforming raw data into meaningful insights. I enjoy exploring data, identifying patterns, building dashboards, and presenting information in a way that is simple and easy to understand.

My current toolkit includes Excel, SQL, and Power BI, and I’m continuously developing my skills through practical projects and hands-on learning. My portfolio showcases projects where I apply data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and dashboard development to real-world datasets.

Beyond data analytics, I’m a creative entrepreneur and the founder of BUMAT Fashion, where I explore fashion, styling, and creativity. This combination of analytical and creative interests has shaped the way I approach problems with curiosity, attention to detail, and a willingness to keep learning.

I’m currently building my experience in data analytics and looking forward to opportunities where I can learn, contribute, and grow.
